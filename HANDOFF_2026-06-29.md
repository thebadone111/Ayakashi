## 6. Key sampler insight (for reference)

Why Euler + simple everywhere: Wan 2.2 and all modern diffusion models (FLUX, SD3) use **flow matching** — the ODE paths between noise and image are nearly straight lines. Euler's linear step follows those straight paths exactly. Higher-order solvers (DPM++2M, Heun, etc.) were designed for DDPM's curved ODE paths and add unnecessary computation for flow matching. ModelSamplingSD3 shift=8 adjusts the timestep schedule so the sigma mapping is correct at low step counts.
