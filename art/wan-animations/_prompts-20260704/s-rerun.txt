An aged bronze temple bell fills the center of the frame, its engraved
surface and dark rope hanging still at rest. The bell faces the viewer
flat-on, neither tilting nor rotating. There is no fire, no smoke, and no
flames anywhere in the frame at any time. The bell gently sways side to
side in a slow narrow arc, gradually returning to center each time, the
rope above swaying softly with a slight lag. A soft golden shimmer
continuously drifts slowly across the bronze surface from one edge to the
other and back. A faint pale-blue foxfire glow at the bell rim steadily
breathes outward then constricts inward. Sparse tiny cyan wisps drift
slowly upward from the rim and dissolve. The motion returns to rest and
the cycle repeats seamlessly. Static locked camera. No camera movement.
