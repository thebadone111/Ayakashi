## 4. My thoughts on the betting bar — what would make it genuinely pop

The bar works mechanically. Here's what separates "functional" from "polished":

### 4.1. The pill needs ground

Right now the dark pill sits directly on the misty game background. It works but looks floated. AoA and most Stake games solve this with **a full-width translucent base strip** — a Rectangle that spans the full game width, roughly 110px tall, with `alpha=0.55`, `backgroundColor=0x000000` or a dark gradient. The pill then floats on top of this strip. Net effect: the bar has a "floor" and is always legible regardless of what the background is doing.

Implementation: add one wide low-alpha Rectangle behind the pill in LayoutDesktop, spanning `x=0` to `TOTAL_WIDTH` at `MID`, full game width.

### 4.2. The spin button deserves a glow ring

The white circle is clean but silent. A very faint gold pulse (scale tween from 1.0→1.04→1.0 on a 1.5s loop, with the ring fading from `0xffd700` at alpha 0.2→0) on a separate ring sprite behind the SPIN button would draw the eye exactly where you want it without being visually noisy. This is a 20-line ticker addition.

### 4.3. Active turbo mode should be more obvious

Currently the active bolt changes (outline → solid → gold) but the button background stays the same dark circle. Consider: in mode 1 and 2, the circle border lights up gold (`borderColor=0xffd700`, `borderWidth=3`). UiButton already has `active` prop wired to `borderColor` — just make sure `active = speedMode > 0` is passed correctly (it already is via `ButtonTurbo.svelte`).

### 4.4. The BET readout should invite interaction

"BET $1.00" currently looks identical to BALANCE and WIN. Players might not realise they can tap it. Small cues that help: slightly brighter fill on the value text (`fill=0xffffff` vs `0xdddddd` for the others), or a very faint rounded rect underline/highlight on the bet value only. Minimal code change.

### 4.5. Arrow hit targets are tiny

The `▲▼` arrows at `scale=0.38` have a hit region of ~57px rendered. On mobile this is borderline. For desktop it's fine, but worth noting for a future mobile pass. The `eventMode` and hit area extension in PixiJS (`button.hitArea = new Rectangle(...)`) can solve this without changing visuals.

### 4.6. Icon contrast at dark-on-dark

The icons are black/dark images on a dark `0x2e2e48` circle. In the hidden-tab screenshot they appeared as very dark circles. In a real lit browser they should contrast more (the background is `0x2e2e48` = dark blue-grey, icons are black — contrast is low). Consider: tint the sprites white (`tint=0xffffff`) in UiButton when icon renders on dark backgrounds. This is a one-liner: `<Sprite ... tint={0xffffff} />`. Would immediately make all icons pop as bright white icons on the dark buttons — much more readable.
